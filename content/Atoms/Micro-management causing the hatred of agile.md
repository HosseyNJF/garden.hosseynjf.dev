[[Agile methodology]] seems like a nice framework to organize engineering work, and can benefit everyone involved, from developers to managers and product owners. Yet many engineers say they hate it.

In my opinion, the issue arises from the thirst of micro-managing engineers from some high-impact product managers and stakeholders. The idea that now I can squeeze as much as possible from my developers seems too appealing to them. This caused the formation of a collective experience that agile leads to [[Micro-Management]].
